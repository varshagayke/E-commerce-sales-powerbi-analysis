# E-Commerce Sales Analysis – Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI analysis of e-commerce sales data**. The dashboard analyzes overall sales and profit performance, regional performance, product categories, orders, customers, and shipping costs.

The project is designed to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards and visualizations.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze total sales and profit
* Understand sales performance across product categories
* Analyze regional and state-level performance
* Examine total orders and customers
* Analyze shipping costs and their impact on profitability
* Identify key business insights from the data

## 📌 Dashboard Pages

### 1. E-Commerce Analysis

The main dashboard provides an overview of:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Total Customers
* Sales by Category
* Profit by Category
* Region and Category filters

The dashboard reports approximately **$1.53M in total sales, $801K in profit, 11K orders, and 4K customers**.

### 2. Regional Analysis

The regional dashboard analyzes sales and profit across regions and states.

It includes:

* Regional sales
* Regional profit
* Total orders
* State-level performance
* Geographic visualization
* Region and category filters

The dashboard also provides detailed state-level performance within regions.

### 3. Shipping Analysis

The shipping dashboard focuses on shipping efficiency and its relationship with profitability.

Key metrics include:

* Shipping Cost %
* Shipping Cost per Order
* Shipping Cost per Unit
* Profit After Shipping
* Shipping cost by category
* Quantity and shipping cost comparison

The dashboard reports a **30.42% shipping cost percentage** and approximately **$335.8K profit after shipping**.

### 4. Business Insights

The final dashboard summarizes important findings from the analysis, including category concentration, regional performance, and shipping efficiency.

Key observations include:

* Food and Disposables contribute strongly to sales and profit.
* Regional performance varies significantly.
* Shipping costs can affect profitability, particularly for high-volume categories.
* Regional and category concentration may create dependency on specific markets and product categories.
